PURPOSE
-------
Digital Mana Badi is a reusable, school-focused digital platform intended to
support school administration, teachers, parents, alumni and sponsors.
The repository is the master codebase. School-specific settings are managed
through config/school-config.js, with a separate Firebase project intended
for each school.

CORE WORKING PRINCIPLES
-----------------------
1. Preserve all working functions. Never break or remove existing behavior
   while improving the interface.
2. Make focused, backward-compatible changes. Avoid unnecessary rewrites.
3. Preserve authentication, role access, navigation, modules, integrations,
   social ribbon and footer behavior unless a change is explicitly requested.
4. Routine, in-scope changes may proceed through the established branch/PR
   workflow without repeatedly asking for approval.
5. Ask before major scope changes or destructive/security-sensitive decisions.
6. Do not claim browser/visual testing unless it was actually performed.
7. Keep the experience mobile-first, premium, readable and free of clipping
   or unwanted horizontal scrolling.
8. User-facing login errors must remain generic and friendly. Never expose
   Firebase internals, implementation details or sensitive role restrictions.

PROJECT STRUCTURE / ARCHITECTURE
-------------------------------
- Master repository supports multiple schools.
- School-specific configuration: config/school-config.js
- Role areas:
  super-admin/
  head-master/
  teacher/
  parent/
  alumni-sponsor/
- Shared reusable code belongs in js/shared/ or js/modules/.
- Follow gradual migration; do not rewrite working modules just to reorganize.
- Keep school-specific Firebase projects separate.
